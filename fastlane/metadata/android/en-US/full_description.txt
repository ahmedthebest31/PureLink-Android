Pure Link is the ultimate "Swiss Army Knife" for privacy-conscious users and developers. It is a powerful, Ultra-Lightweight URL cleaner that strips tracking parameters, expands shortened links, opens direct chats, and provides developer tools — wrapped in a Sci-Fi themed, screen-reader optimized interface.

🛡️ PRIVACY & CORE SANITIZATION

* Flexible Usage: Clean links manually in the app, share links directly to Pure Link from other apps, or enable the optional background monitoring service for full automation.
* Deep Cleaning & Invisible Processing: Share any link from any app. It instantly removes tracking tags (utm_, fbclid, gclid, ref) and copies the clean URL to your clipboard without opening a new screen.
* In-Place Text Cleaning: Select any text or URL inside any app, tap "Pure Link" from the context menu, and it instantly cleans and replaces the link right where you type.
* Smart Unshortener & Advanced Resolution: Safely expands shortened URLs (e.g., bit.ly) in the background to reveal the true destination, simulating standard browser requests to resolve complex redirect chains and protect against hidden malicious sites.
* Auto-Updating Filters: Automatically fetches the latest tracking filters from our GitHub repository weekly, keeping privacy protection up to date without an app update.

📜 SMART WORKFLOW & TOOLS

* Quick Settings Tile: Toggle the optional clipboard monitoring service on or off from your phone's notification shade.
* 3-Tab Navigation: Switch between Dashboard (stats & history), Tools (Base64, UUID, chat), and Settings.
* Smart Commands: Type /wa, /tg, /b64e, /b64d, /uuid, /upper, /lower, /capitalize, /reverse, /clear, or /trim followed by text for instant processing.
* Ignore List, YouTube Shorts conversion, bilingual EN/AR UI, smart history (last 10 cleaned links), direct messaging, Base64/UUID utilities, and 7 themes (Matrix, Amber CRT, Dracula, Monokai, Light, High Contrast, System).

♿ ACCESSIBILITY FIRST (Screen Reader Optimized)

* Designed by a Blind Developer: Built from the ground up with a "Blind-First" approach, ensuring 100% compatibility with TalkBack and other screen readers. Every element is clearly labeled.
* WCAG AA Compliance: Proper semantic roles (Button, Switch, TextInput) on all interactive elements, merged descendants for compound widgets, and content descriptions for every control.
* WCAG AA Contrast: All text colors meet minimum contrast ratios against their backgrounds in both Matrix and Light themes.
* Smart Feedback: Precise details and haptic vibrations after every action (e.g., "2 links cleaned, 1 unshortened").
* Sci-Fi Theme & Maximum Efficiency: Sleek "Green on Black" terminal style for energy efficiency; optimized to run indefinitely without draining battery or system resources.
